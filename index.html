How to play

Place: Pick one unused tile, rotate it, then tap a legal empty hex. After the first tile, placements must touch an existing tile by a full edge.
D tile: The six-way tile cannot be played on either of your first two personal turns. It unlocks on your third turn.
Score: Complete a closed route with exactly six direction changes. The player whose placement completes it scores 1 point.
Sides: A side may continue straight through several tiles. The six sides do not have to be equal lengths.
Finish: The game ends when everyone has used all 16 tiles, or no legal placements remain. Highest score wins.
