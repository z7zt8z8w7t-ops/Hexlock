How to play

  1. Blue starts. Place one counter on an empty hex. Your move must flip at least one yellow counter.
  2. Flip a line when one or more opponent counters lie in a straight line between your new counter and one already yours. Check all six directions and flip every line you trap.
  3. Pass automatically if you cannot flip. The other player takes another turn.
  4. Finish when neither player can move, or the board is full. Most counters wins; equal counts tie.
  5. Vs computer: You play blue. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard. Undo takes back your move and the computer's reply.
